## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Documentary scope and explicit activation boundary

The design SHALL separate specified future guards from implemented or activated
behavior and retain version-16 native authority over transactions and permissions.

#### Scenario: Deliver the coexistence specification

- **WHEN** this design is validated
- **THEN** only documentary files SHALL change
- **AND** no hook, client script, site record, permission or integration SHALL be modified
- **AND** transactional tests and operational activation SHALL remain pending

### Requirement: Authoritative domain without fictional isolation

The design SHALL identify company-scoped restaurant adjustments separately from
existing primary-profile restrictions and incomplete contexts.

#### Scenario: A separate non-restaurant company uses POS

- **WHEN** a company without Restaurant Company Settings is considered
- **THEN** future restaurant series and expense guards SHALL be specified as no-ops
- **AND** native naming, invoice validation and closing SHALL remain authoritative
- **AND** no restaurant settings SHALL be created as a workaround

#### Scenario: A draft lacks company or profile

- **WHEN** the context is incomplete or asynchronous responses are stale
- **THEN** the design SHALL forbid using the user's default company to select a domain
- **AND** contradictory company/profile/opening values SHALL require rejection
- **AND** incomplete drafts SHALL retain native mandatory-field controls

#### Scenario: Multiple operations share a company

- **WHEN** restaurant and minimarket profiles belong to the same company
- **THEN** company settings SHALL NOT be represented as profile isolation
- **AND** a separate approved profile classification and legacy-profile decision SHALL be required

### Requirement: Preserve global invoice mode and native fallback

The design SHALL preserve POS Settings as site-global and cover server and client
guards, native delegation and existing restaurant opt-ins together.

#### Scenario: Sales Invoice is proposed for only one company

- **WHEN** a per-company invoice mode is suggested
- **THEN** the design SHALL document that the standard setting cannot provide it
- **AND** changing the singleton or closing unrelated sessions SHALL remain excluded

#### Scenario: A non-restaurant closing is evaluated

- **WHEN** future guards and class overrides are specified
- **THEN** native validators SHALL execute outside restaurant scope
- **AND** expense scripts SHALL NOT recalculate its reconciliation or replace native summaries
- **AND** regression SHALL include restaurant expenses, secondary profiles and permanent-opening opt-ins

### Requirement: Temporary context and evidence are controlled

The design SHALL prohibit bypass flags and manual commits and require restoration
of any necessary temporary flag to its exact previous state.

#### Scenario: A future helper succeeds or raises

- **WHEN** implementation introduces temporary context
- **THEN** regression SHALL cover restoration on success, failure and reentry
- **AND** absent flags SHALL remain absent and existing values SHALL be preserved
- **AND** pure tests SHALL NOT be described as site or fiscal acceptance
